# Breast Imaging Research Model Card

## Status
Research-only. No imaging model artifact is currently approved or deployed.

## Safety
This work is not an autonomous clinical diagnostic tool. Raw scores are never shown to patients. Radiologist interpretation is required. Evaluation must include calibration, false-negative/false-positive review, subgroup results, and external-test results before any research review.

## Data governance
Only explicitly approved, de-identified research DICOM datasets are eligible. Patient archive studies, chats, reports, and uploaded documents are prohibited inputs.
